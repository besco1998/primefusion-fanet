# Bibliography Analysis - Blockchain in UAV Communication

## Key Research Areas Identified

### 1. Routing and Trust Management
- **Jia et al. (2025)**: Trusted routing via MARL and blockchain trust management
- **He et al. (2025)**: Zero-trust architecture for LAINs with routing optimization
- **Luo et al. (2023)**: ESCM - Routing + blockchain + PoNC consensus

### 2. Authentication and Identity Management
- **Jiao et al. (2025)**: Lightweight authentication using aSVC
- **Hafeez et al. (2024)**: BETA-UAV - Blockchain-based ECC authentication
- **Dong et al. (2023)**: Multi-cluster UAV identity authentication

### 3. Consensus Mechanisms
- **Huang et al. (2025)**: HotStuff consensus performance in UAV networks
- **Han et al. (2024)**: DTPBFT - Dynamic PBFT for UAV swarms
- **Hossain et al. (2024)**: Private blockchain with PBFT-like consensus

### 4. Security and Defense
- **Ajakwe et al. (2025)**: iBANDA - Blockchain defense for drone logistics
- **Jagatheesaperumal et al. (2023)**: Cross-layer security architecture for 5G/6G UAVs
- **Jin (2024)**: Continuous authentication with reputation mechanism

### 5. Post-Disaster and Emergency Response
- **Hafeez et al. (2024)**: Consortium blockchain with DPoS-PBFT for multi-agency coordination
- **Khor et al. (2025)**: LoRa D2D with blockchain UTM for collision avoidance

### 6. Comprehensive Surveys
- **Hafeez et al. (2023)**: IEEE OJVT comprehensive survey (81 citations)
- **Hawashin (2024)**: Industry review (46 citations)

## Key Technologies and Approaches

### Blockchain Types
- **Public**: SHA256-based (Khor et al.), ECC-based (Hafeez BETA-UAV)
- **Permissioned**: HotStuff, PBFT variants, aSVC-based
- **Consortium**: DPoS-PBFT hybrid (Hafeez post-disaster)
- **Private**: PBFT-like (Hossain et al.)

### Consensus Mechanisms
- **Proof-of-Network-Coding (PoNC)**: Luo et al. ESCM
- **HotStuff**: Huang et al. performance analysis
- **PBFT variants**: DTPBFT (Han), DPoS-PBFT (Hafeez)
- **Proof-of-Cooperation**: Potential for PrimeFusion

### Communication Technologies
- **LoRa**: Long-range D2D communication
- **WiFi**: 2.4/5 GHz mesh networks
- **5G/6G**: Next-generation UAV communication
- **FSO**: Free-space optical links
